About Al-Noor Academy

Building Generations of God-Conscious Scholars & Innovators

Founded in 2010, Al-Noor Academy was established with a singular vision: to bridge the gap between sacred Islamic tradition and modern secular academic excellence.

Our Vision

To be an international model of educational excellence, nurturing well-rounded citizens who lead with integrity, knowledge, and Islamic faith.

Our Mission

To provide an enriching environment that combines rigorous academic standards with authentic Islamic Tarbiyah, encouraging critical thinking, creativity, and moral excellence.

🕌 Sacred Learning

Deen Curriculum

In-depth Islamic learning structured for young minds to practice and cherish Islam every single day.

Qur'an & Tajweed

Correct pronunciation, recitation rules, memorization (Hifz/Nazira), and understanding key verses.

Daily Duas & Prayer

Practical Salah training, daily Sunnah supplications, and morning/evening Adhkar routines.

Arabic Language

Building fluent Quranic Arabic vocabulary, reading comprehension, and basic conversational skills.

Akhlaq & Character

Tarbiyah focused on honesty, humility, respect for parents/teachers, and community empathy.

Seerah & History

Inspiring stories from the life of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, the Companions, and Islamic heritage.

Islamic Studies

Age-appropriate Fiqh of daily life, Aqeedah (theology), and applying Islamic solutions to modern issues.

Congregational Prayer

Daily Zuhr prayer in congregation inside our school Masjid with student-led Adhan.

Hifz Support Track

Flexible learning tracks for students aiming to complete Quran memorization alongside academics.
